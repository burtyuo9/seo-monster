### Pricing Comparison

**Popolnyator** offers a transparent and straightforward fee structure with competitive rates and no hidden charges. The instant issuance and global acceptance come without extra premium fees, making it ideal for users who want hassle-free, affordable access to crypto card services.

**Crypto.com** operates on a tiered pricing model based on CRO token staking. While the base card has minimal fees, unlocking higher cashback rates and perks requires staking significant amounts of CRO tokens for a fixed period (usually 6 months). This can result in higher upfront costs or opportunity costs for users. Additionally, some card tiers have annual fees or activation fees, depending on the region.

In summary, Popolnyator is generally more cost-effective for users who want straightforward pricing without staking commitments, while Crypto.com is geared towards those willing to invest in staking for premium benefits.

### Security Comparison

Security is paramount when dealing with digital assets, and both Popolnyator and Crypto.com take it seriously.

- **Popolnyator** complies with PCI DSS standards, ensuring secure handling of cardholder data. The use of 256-bit SSL encryption protects user data during transactions. While Popolnyator emphasizes secure instant issuance and global acceptance, it is less transparent about cold storage or insurance policies for crypto holdings.

- **Crypto.com** has a well-documented security framework including PCI DSS compliance, two-factor authentication (2FA), cold wallet storage for the majority of crypto assets, insurance coverage against theft, and regular third-party audits (SOC 2 Type 2). These measures provide a higher level of assurance for users with larger crypto balances or who prioritize institutional-grade security.
