## What Is an Anonymous Virtual Credit Card?

An **anonymous virtual credit card** is a digital payment card that functions like a traditional credit or debit card but does not require the cardholder to disclose personal identity information. Unlike physical cards, virtual cards exist only in digital form and can be used for online purchases, subscriptions, and payments wherever card payments are accepted.

### How It Differs From Traditional Cards

- **No Physical Card:** Issued instantly without the need for plastic.
- **Enhanced Privacy:** Minimal or no personal identification required.
- **Single-Use or Reloadable:** Can be disposable or reusable for multiple transactions.
- **Instant Issuance:** Created and funded quickly online.

When combined with cryptocurrency funding, these cards offer a seamless bridge between crypto assets and fiat currency payments.
